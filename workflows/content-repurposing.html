Content · STARTER WORKFLOW

Adapt one useful article into three grounded social posts.

Extract real substance rather than recycling a headline. Tie every draft post directly to a claim the original article actually makes.

Proposed starter workflow · Untested · Preview for editorial review rather than a validated case study.

What this builds

You will produce three draft social posts from an article you wrote or hold permission to adapt: a step-by-step checklist, a focused explanation, and a candid discussion question. This proposed starter workflow is untested. It does not schedule or publish content, create visual assets, or guarantee reach.

01 / Map claims to source paragraphs

Number the paragraphs in your article before pasting it into your AI tool. Define the target reader and the specific takeaway you want them to leave with. Ask the model to propose three distinct angles and cite the exact paragraph numbers supporting each one. Discard any angle that relies on outside metrics, invented stories, or stronger conclusions than your source text can back up.

Work from your own writing, or make sure you have the right to adapt the piece. As YouTube’s copyright guidance explains, adaptations can be derivative works where permission or licensing may matter. Giving credit is not the same as checking whether you need permission.

02 / Assign each post a distinct angle

Give each draft a distinct job so you aren't just rewriting the same hook three times. Have the checklist break one specific task into clear steps. Have the explanation answer one narrow question in plain language. Have the discussion post name a real trade-off from the piece rather than asking a generic engagement question.

If you want a slide or carousel outline, ask for five simple frames: question, context, method, limitation, and takeaway. Keep the copy brief enough to scan on a phone without trimming the caveats that keep the advice accurate. Treat any visual ideas from the model as a rough brief until you make or license real assets.

03 / Audit against the source text

Check each draft side by side against its cited paragraphs. Strip out invented first-person anecdotes, sweeping promises, fabricated quotes, or implied tests you never ran. Swap generic hype for a specific detail from the text. When your article says an approach "may help," do not let the social draft escalate that into "will transform."

Before you post

Test every link, check current platform formatting rules, preview the layout on a phone, and write helpful alt text for any images. Check rights and permissions for any visuals or music you use. Publish manually after your review is done, and save the final published text alongside the source article so later updates to the piece don't leave an outdated claim circulating.

Starter prompt

Fill in the bracketed placeholders and remove any sensitive details before pasting into an AI tool.

Adapt the article below for [audience] on [platform]. First, list three distinct, useful angles and cite the numbered source paragraphs that support each one. Next, draft three separate posts: (1) a practical checklist for one task, (2) a plain-language explanation of one narrow concept, and (3) a grounded discussion question centered on a real trade-off. Keep every claim within the exact limits and certainty of the original text. Do not add personal anecdotes, unverified statistics, implied testing, quotes, or guaranteed outcomes. Flag any missing context in brackets. Add a private note under each draft showing which paragraph numbers it draws from. Do not publish or schedule anything. Article: [paste numbered paragraphs].
