03 / EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Verifiable beats
flashy.

01

Start from the work.

Start with the actual job, not a novelty tool. When an AI step adds extra review work without making the task clearer, skip it.

02

Keep evidence traceable.

Fluent prose is not proof. Keep raw inputs, source links, and verification steps in plain sight so you can inspect every claim.

03

Label untested ideas.

Our starter workflows are untested editorial proposals, not validated benchmarks. Try them on low-risk work and verify the results yourself.

04

Hold the final pen.

Models can sort notes and propose drafts, but business decisions, sensitive data handling, and published claims remain your responsibility.
